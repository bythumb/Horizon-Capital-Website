Who Qualifies
Eligible degrees.

This program is designed for licensed medical professionals and qualifying advanced degree holders:

MD DO DDS DMD PharmD DVM VMD DPM CRNA
Eligibility based on profession, income, credit, and overall borrower profile. CRNA requires qualifying doctoral degree.
Additional Features

First-time homebuyers may be eligible for the program — ideal for residents and fellows making their first purchase.

Reserves
