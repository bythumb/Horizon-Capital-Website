I began my career in the mortgage industry in 2020. After two years of learning the business and helping clients achieve homeownership, I used that same expertise to purchase my own home. Every loan has its own complexity, and I pride myself on breaking that down into a language you can understand. I specialize in helping first-time homebuyers navigate their options and choose the loan product that truly fits their situation — not just the one that looks good on paper.

I'm proud to be part of Horizon Capital Lending Group, where we offer something rare in this industry: real estate, loans, and escrow all under one roof. Through their exclusive Save & Bundle program, I'm able to get my clients better rates and a smoother, more connected experience from start to finish.
