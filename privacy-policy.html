Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who We Are

Horizon Capital Lending Group is a division of First Link Mortgage Inc. ("First Link Mortgage," "we," "us," or "our"), a California-licensed mortgage broker (NMLS #2527988, CA DRE #01527812) located at 31630 Railroad Canyon Rd, Suite 15, Canyon Lake, CA 92587. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide through our website, including information submitted through our contact and pre-qualification forms, and how we handle text messaging ("SMS") opt-in data.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form on our website, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Property address and loan-related details you choose to provide
  • Any message or information you voluntarily enter into a form field
  • Your SMS opt-in choices (whether you consented to non-marketing and/or marketing text messages)

We retain this information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, service an active loan file, or maintain a record of your consent status, and no longer than necessary for those purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, process pre-qualification or loan requests, schedule appointments, and communicate with you about your financing options. If you opt in to receive text messages, we use your phone number solely to send the type of messages you consented to receive, as described below.

SMS & Text Messaging Communications

If you check a consent box on one of our forms, you agree to receive text messages from First Link Mortgage at the phone number you provide. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us; you will receive one final confirmation message and will not receive further SMS messages from us. Reply HELP at any time for assistance, or contact us using the information at the bottom of this page.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Data Security

Information submitted through our website is transmitted using industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS) and is stored in systems with access limited to authorized personnel. We use GoHighLevel as our customer communications and relationship management platform to receive and manage form submissions and SMS messaging. We may also share information with wholesale lending partners as necessary to process a loan application you have requested, in accordance with this policy.

Your Choices

You may decline to provide your phone number or decline either SMS consent checkbox and still submit a form or contact us by phone or email. You may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP, or by contacting us directly using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our website and SMS program are intended for individuals 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at conrad@horizonclg.com, call (951) 970-9107, or write to us at 31630 Railroad Canyon Rd, Suite 15, Canyon Lake, CA 92587.
